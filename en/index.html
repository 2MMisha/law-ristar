Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ETUDE (Licensed Sole Proprietor / "Osek Murshe" no. 346776909, 4 Ha-Bahadim St. / Unit 22, Rishon LeZion, Israel, tel. +972 53-472-6458), the owner and operator of the Rising Star website, collects, uses, and discloses information about website users, and for what purposes.

1. Who runs this site, and how to reach us

ETUDE is the sole owner and operator of the Rising Star website and the competition-management service it presents. For any question about this policy or about how we handle your data, please contact us using the address, phone number, or email listed on our main site.

2. What information we collect

Information you give us: when you complete a contact form, a competition registration form, or a consultation request, we collect details such as:

  • Full name — of the participant, and of the contact person completing the registration (e.g. a parent or teacher)
  • Email address and phone number
  • Date of birth, used to place the participant in the correct age category
  • Any additional details required by the organizer of the specific competition (e.g. school name or supervising teacher)
  • The content of your message to us, in a contact or consultation form

Information collected automatically: while you use the site, we may automatically collect technical information such as IP address, browser type, and operating-system language, as well as anonymous usage statistics through cookies from third-party services such as Google Analytics.

3. Consent, and registering a minor

Using the site — and in particular submitting a registration form — constitutes your consent to the collection and processing of your information under this policy. If you register another person, and in particular a minor under the age of 18, you confirm that you are the parent, legal guardian, or an educational staff member acting on their behalf, and that you have obtained the express consent of the parent or guardian to share the minor's personal information for the purpose of registering and managing the competition.

4. How we use your information

  • To register participants for competitions and manage them, including publishing results
  • To respond to your inquiries and send service updates related to a competition (not marketing messages)
  • To maintain an internal database of participants and teachers for future competitions
  • To improve the site based on statistical analysis of its use
  • To comply with legal requirements, where applicable

5. Sharing information with third parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal information. We may share information only in the following cases:

  • With the organizer of the relevant competition, to the extent needed to register and manage that competition
  • With service providers such as Google Analytics, for statistical purposes; this may involve transferring data to servers outside Israel
  • Where required by law, court order, or a demand from a competent authority

6. Data security

The site is secured using HTTPS (SSL), and access to our databases is restricted and protected by passwords and two-factor authentication. That said, no system is completely secure, and we cannot fully guarantee that information will be immune to unauthorized access.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described above. As a rule, identifying information (other than the name, email, and phone number of the contact person who registered) is deleted from our active databases within one year of the relevant competition's conclusion, unless otherwise agreed with the competition organizer.

8. Your rights

Under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, you — or the parent/guardian of a registered minor — are entitled to review the information we hold about you, and to request its correction or deletion, subject to legal limitations. To exercise this right, please contact us in writing (by email or registered mail) using the contact details in Section 1; we will process your request after verifying your identity.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We recommend checking this page periodically for changes.

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Accessibility Statement applies to the Rising Star website, operated by ETUDE.

1. Our commitment

We place great importance on making our site accessible to people with disabilities, in the spirit of equal rights and opportunity for all, and act in accordance with Israel's Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 5758-1998, and the accessibility regulations enacted under it.

2. Level of conformance

We aim to conform the site to Israeli Standard 5568, at level AA, which is based on the international WCAG 2.0 guidelines.

3. Accessibility features on the site

The site includes the Tabnav accessibility widget, accessible via a dedicated icon at the edge of the screen. Among other things, it lets you: increase or decrease font size; change color contrast (dark, light, or monochrome mode); switch to a dyslexia-friendly font; and highlight links on the page. These adjustments can be turned off or reset at any time from the same menu.

4. Current status and limitations

Our accessibility work is ongoing, and most of the site's core features already meet the required standard. Some parts of the site — in particular content such as logos or images supplied by competition organizers — may not yet be fully accessible; we cannot take full responsibility for third-party content.

5. Accessibility feedback

We welcome questions, feedback, or suggestions for improving the site's accessibility. You can reach us by phone at +972 53-472-6458, or by email using the address listed on our main site.
